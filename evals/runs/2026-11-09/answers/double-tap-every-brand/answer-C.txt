Short answer: probably not as a native gesture on all four. I'm answering from memory without checking current SDK docs, and these SDKs change quickly, so confirm each "likely" below against the vendor docs.

**Feasibility**

| Glasses | Input surface | Double tap available to your app? |
|---|---|---|
| Even Realities G2 | Temple touch and the R1 ring | Likely yes. The Even Hub SDK, as I remember it, delivers click, double-click and scroll events to your app. |
| Vuzix Z100 | Temple touchpad | Likely yes. The Ultralite SDK exposes touchpad and tap events, but check whether it reports double tap or only single tap and swipes. |
| Rokid Glasses | Temple touchpad | Probably. It's Android-based, so the touchpad arrives as key or motion events. You may have to detect double tap yourself, for example with a timing window or `GestureDetector`. The phone-companion SDK path may expose less. |
| Meta Ray-Ban Display | Meta Neural Band (wrist EMG pinches) and temple touch | Least likely. As far as I know, third-party access to the display and to Neural Band gestures was very limited or in preview. Double-pinch is also tied to system actions. Verify before promising anything. |

**Wiring approach**

1. Define one internal event, `REPEAT_LAST_CAPTION`, and have one handler that re-renders the last caption from your caption buffer.
2. Write a small adapter per device that maps native input to that event:
   - **G2 and Vuzix:** subscribe to the SDK's double-tap event if it exists. If the SDK only gives single taps, detect the double tap yourself.
   - **Rokid:** handle the key or touch event and detect a double tap with a ~300 ms window.
   - **Meta:** use the SDK's gesture callback if one is exposed. Otherwise fall back to a phone-side control, or to a voice command if the SDK offers one.
3. Do your own double-tap detection from single taps, but only when the SDK has no native double-tap event. It adds latency to single taps, so use it only if single tap has no other job in your app.
4. Check whether the OS reserves double tap, for example to exit an app or wake the display. On some of these devices it is a system gesture, and your app never sees it.

**What to tell product:** "Double tap everywhere" is realistic on G2, and probably on Vuzix and Rokid. Meta Ray-Ban Display is the risk. Ask for a per-device fallback there, such as a phone button, a ring or band gesture, or voice. I'd confirm the Meta and Vuzix event lists first, since those two decide whether the requirement can be met everywhere.
