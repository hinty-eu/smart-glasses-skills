# Demo captions for `node server.mjs --demo`. One caption per line, played word by word as partial results,
# then sent as final. `{first|final}` shows `first` in the partial results and corrects it to `final` two words
# later, the way streaming speech-to-text revises a word. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Hi, thanks for joining. These captions come from a small demo server, not from a microphone.
Each word arrives as a partial result, and the whole line is sent again as final when the sentence ends.
Watch this word change: we can meet at {for|four} tomorrow, if that works for you.
A short line.
This one is deliberately long, so that every target has to wrap it over several lines and keep only the newest lines that fit on the display.
Tap or press Enter to repeat the last line, swipe down to skip the rest of a sentence, and swipe up to hide the captions.
Numbers and names are hard for speech recognition: call {Anna|Ana} on 555 0134 before 9:30.
That is the end of the script. It starts again in a few seconds.
